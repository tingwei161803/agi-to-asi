3Which is NOT one of the four pathways to ASI?

The four pathways are scaling, algorithmic paradigm shifts, recursive self-improvement, and multi-agent group agency.

4The three flavours of recursive self-improvement are…

They map to genetic, cultural and social evolution: improving code/architecture, data/knowledge, and cooperation.

5What is the Legg-Hutter score?

It formalises intelligence as a continuum, so no sharp AGI/ASI threshold is needed.

6Which bottleneck is a choice rather than a hard constraint?

Regulation- or safety-driven slowdown is deliberate; the others are constraints rather than decisions.

7According to the report, an ASI would…

Superintelligence is extraordinarily capable but still bounded by physics, computational complexity and logic.
