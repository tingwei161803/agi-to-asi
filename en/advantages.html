Table 1 · Digital intelligence

Advantages of a digital mind

Properties of digital intelligence that scale with compute and hardware in ways biology cannot — widening the human–AI gap as computers grow more powerful.

Faster

Internal processing speed

Digital minds can be sped up, slowed, or paused, and run on far shorter timescales than biological neurons.

Perfect

Lossless replication

Source code and full memory state copy exactly — spin up large numbers of identical instances.

Vast

Working memory

Far larger working memory than biological minds.

High

Input / output speed

Collect and emit data far faster than human senses and motor output.

High-bandwidth experience sharing

Instances exchange experience at high bandwidth; many lifetimes can be simulated and replayed to fine-tune a specialist.

Portable

Substrate independence

The same AI runs on any sufficiently powerful computer; moving to new hardware is comparatively easy.
